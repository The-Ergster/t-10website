Who we are

T-10 is the Varsity robotics team at Needham High School. The club operates all year during the school year, meeting Tuesdays and Thursdays for four hours per week. We're dedicated to fostering STEM education through the FIRST Tech Challenge (FTC) program — a year-long robotics program that combines engineering, coding, documentation, professionalism, and outreach to prepare students to be future STEM leaders.

Mission statement

To empower high school students to pursue STEM by developing problem-solving, collaboration, and real-world skills through competitive robotics.

Our goals

Offer all high school students the free opportunity to explore their STEM passions while building competitive, successful FTC teams within the Needham Robotics Club.
